10 Steps to Successful Buying

To be successful in buying you a nice place, I follow 10 steps. I will be your personal guide!

Step 1. Intake

We meet in person and discuss your personal preferences. We define who is buying, what you are searching for, where you are searching, the available budget, your mortgage-advisor and the planning.

Step 2. Agreement

When we are both committed, we sign the agreement: on the basis of 'no cure no pay'.

Step 3. Search and Viewings

I accompany you to view shortlisted properties and provide on-site advice about the type of house, it's condition and market value to ensure you need all the info you need about the property.

Step 4. Research

If you really like the property, I advise you by studying the available documents: the status of the Owners Association, the available minutes; the condition of the house; the legal issues; etc.

Step 5. Negotiation

If you are ready to bid, I advice you on the price; the bidding conditions; a construction research; the notary; the time of delivery; a deposit/bank-guarantee. I will hand in the bid in time and keep in touch with the selling agent.

Step 6. Signing the Purchase Deed

After we have a mutual agreement with the selling party, I guide you on the first time visit at the notary. Beforehand we study the concept-deed, we discuss the date of deposit, the date of unbinding conditions and the date of delivery.

Step 7. Financing

After signing the buying contract, the financing process starts: I will arrange a valuation report, which will be send to your mortgage-advisor. On basis of this valuation report and the signed buying contract, your mortgage advisor applies for your mortgage at different banks. After receiving the available offers, you choose the best offer, in cooperation with your mortgage-advisor.

1 week before delivery-date the notary sends you the final settlement including the costs for the notary, the transfer tax, the municipality taxes, the broker fee, the costs for joining the Owners Association. This total amount due has to be transferred into the account of the notary before the delivery-date.

Step 8. Signing the Delivery Deed

On the day of delivery: I guide you on the second visit at the notary: you sign the delivery-contract and receive the keys. Before signing, we will inspect the apartment together with the seller. The day after delivery, the notary pays all the money to each party involved.

Step 9. Moving

Now you are ready to move. Make sure you have your house-contents insurance. Also, make sure the utility contracts (water, electricity, gas) will be transferred on behalf of your name.

Step 10. Ground Lease

Depending on the ground-situation of the property, I advice and assist you on applying for buying off groundlease.
