Purchasing in Amsterdam is quite a job. Finding a specific property in a desired location can be extremely challenging when relying solely on listing platforms.

## A real-life example

We were looking for an apartment in one specific apartment building on the Amsterdam ring road. Rather than waiting for listings to appear on real estate platforms, we took a direct approach.

By sending personal notes to the residents of the building, we connected with an owner based in New York who was considering a sale. We had enough with this one contact! The deal was closed within 2 weeks.

This demonstrates how personalized outreach can overcome typical market obstacles and accelerate transactions in Amsterdam's competitive real estate market.
