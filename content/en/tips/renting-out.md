Property owners in Amsterdam must ensure their apartments meet specific criteria to avoid rental price restrictions.

The valuation system uses a points-based approach. Several factors determine an apartment's point score, including the WOZ-value, the energy label, the neighborhood, and the other features of the apartment.

Most Amsterdam apartments satisfy minimum point requirements. However, landlords concerned about compliance or seeking to improve their rating can calculate points through the official resource of the rent assessment committee (Huurcommissie).

You can make the calculation of the number of points on the website of the [rent assessment committee](https://www.huurcommissie.nl).
