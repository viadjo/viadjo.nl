A common question from prospective property buyers is whether an annual groundlease fee (canon) affects their maximum mortgage eligibility.

The answer is yes: an annual canon has an impact on the maximum mortgage. However, precise calculations require consultation with a financial advisor.

## Rule of thumb

As a rule of thumb for estimation purposes, you should hold an amount of 20 times the annual ground rent. This calculated figure should be subtracted from what your mortgage would be if no annual groundlease fee existed.
