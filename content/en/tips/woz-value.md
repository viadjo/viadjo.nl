As a homeowner in the Netherlands, you are responsible for paying multiple annual taxes including water/sewage tax, real estate tax, and garbage disposal fees. The municipality calculates these based on a percentage of your property's WOZ-value.

WOZ stands for "Wet Onroerende Zaken" (Immovable Property Act). An important distinction: the WOZ-value is lower than the market-value of your house. In recent years, Amsterdam has experienced significant increases in WOZ valuations.

A notable feature of the Dutch system is transparency -- these valuations are publicly accessible. You can research property values through the official database at [woz-waardeloket.nl](https://www.woz-waardeloket.nl), where you can search by address.
