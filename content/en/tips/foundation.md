A significant portion of Amsterdam's residential structures are constructed on wooden poles. Do not buy a house only on the basis of an issued foundation code, because the foundation code may have been issued a long time ago.

Various factors can compromise a building's foundation over relatively short periods. Standard construction inspections typically don't encompass detailed foundation analysis. Instead, inspectors examine visible cracks that might indicate foundation complications, which may warrant a specialized investigation.

Properties being subdivided into apartment rights must meet foundation code 2 standards.

## Foundation code meanings

- **Code 1:** New construction condition
- **Code 2:** Fair/good condition (no maintenance needed within 25 years)
- **Code 3:** Poor condition (restoration required within 10 years)
- **Code 4:** Bad condition (immediate repair necessary)

Foundation assessment is complex and context-dependent for individual properties.
