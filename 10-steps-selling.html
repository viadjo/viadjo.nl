10 Steps to Successful Selling

For a successful sale of your home, I follow 10 steps. I am your personal guide!

Step 1. Intake

We meet at the property to be sold. We record all details on the basis of a questionnaire and an inspection.

Step 2. Valuation

Based on the intake, the market situation and the transaction prices of comparable homes sold, I determine the value of the home. Together we determine the sales strategy and the asking price.

Step 3. Agreement

Together we sign the service contract. Based on 'no cure no pay'.

Step 4. Sales File

Before the sale can start, a complete file is created. This file contains the following: the VVE documents; the energy label; the title deed; the questionnaire; the list of movables; the NEN measurement report and other relevant information.

Step 5. Photos + Measurement Report

The photographer comes by to take professional photos and a measurement report according to the latest NEN standards.

Step 6. Publication

As soon as the sales file is complete and the photos have been approved, I publish the property on the various online channels and we agree on when the property can be viewed.

Step 7. Sales

Based on the interest and suggestions of the viewers, I advise you on the best sales procedure. You can choose between the regular negotiation process or sales by registration with a deadline.

Step 8. Sign the Purchase Deed

I accompany you during the first visit to the notary. As soon as the house has been sold, an appointment is scheduled for the signing of the deed of sale. In advance we go through the draft deed, determined on the date for the deposit, the reservations and delivery.

Step 9. Delivering the House

Before the date of delivery, the house must be prepared for delivery. All keys, a final check of the list of movables and a cleaning of every room.

Step 10. Sign the Delivery Deed

I accompany you during the second visit to the notary. The deed of delivery is signed and the keys are handed over to the buyers. Before signing, the house is inspected by all parties and the meter readings are noted. The notary transfers the funds to the various parties the day after delivery.
